Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 20, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Tiny Orbit Apps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Guess The Number mobile application (the "App"), a free, ad-supported game. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Username: You may choose an in-app username. If you don't, a random name is generated for you. It is stored on your device, and also in the cloud if you sign in (see below).
  • Google account (optional): If you choose "Sign in with Google" in Settings to back up your progress, we receive your Google account's unique identifier, email address, display name, and profile photo URL through Firebase Authentication.

1.2 Game Data

  • Game statistics: Games played, games won, win streaks, best attempts, and achievement progress.
  • Settings: Language, audio, and notification preferences. They are stored on your device, and also in the cloud if you sign in.

1.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Firebase Analytics collects app opens, session duration, and in-game events such as games started, won, or lost, difficulty selected, and achievements unlocked.
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, app version, app instance and device identifiers, and approximate (country/region-level) location derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, device information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.

1.4 Google Play Games Services

If Google Play Games is available on your device, the App signs you in to it automatically to track achievements. Google Play Games provides your player ID and display name, and stores your achievement progress with your Play Games profile.

The App uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

4. Advertising and Consent

Ads are shown only after any required consent is collected through Google's consent tools. Depending on your region, your consent choices, and your device settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Where a privacy options form is required (for example in the EEA and UK), you can change your choices at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the App.

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • On your device: Game data and settings are stored locally until you use "Reset Progress," clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud backup: If you sign in, your username, statistics, achievements, and settings are stored in Cloud Firestore and kept until you request deletion.
  • Analytics data: Retained by Firebase Analytics according to Google's retention settings (up to 14 months).
  • Advertising data: Retained by Google AdMob according to Google's policies.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With the service providers listed in Section 3, so they can operate features of the App;
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process.

The App has no chat, social features, or public leaderboards, and your username is not shown to other players.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may show daily play and streak reminders. These are scheduled entirely on your device; no external notification service is used. You can turn them off in the App's Settings or your device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete your account and cloud data: See Section 10 below.
  • Delete local data: Use "Reset Progress" in Settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Sign out: Sign out of Google in the App's Settings at any time to stop cloud syncing.
  • Google Play Games: Manage your achievements and profile in your Google Play Games settings.
  • Advertising: Use Settings → Privacy options in the App (where shown), or delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Notifications: Turn them off in the App's Settings or your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, or object to the processing of your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Deleting Your Account and Data

If you signed in with Google, you can ask us to delete your Guess The Number account and cloud data at any time. You don't need the App installed.

  1. Email tinyorbitapps@gmail.com with the subject "Guess The Number - Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, or include that address in the message.
  3. Say whether you want the whole account deleted, or only your game data.

We will complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.

  • Deleted: your Firebase Authentication account (Google account identifier, email address, display name, profile photo URL) and your cloud backup (username, game statistics, achievements, settings).
  • Not linked to your account: Firebase Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. AdMob data follows Google's retention policies.
  • Managed by Google: achievements stored with your Google Play Games profile can be managed in your Google Play Games settings.
  • On your device: local progress is not affected. Use "Reset Progress" in Settings, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.

11. Data Security

All communication with our service providers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS, and cloud data is protected by Firebase Authentication and security rules that restrict each user's data to that user. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where Google's servers are located. We rely on Google's infrastructure and its data protection safeguards for these transfers.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will show changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
